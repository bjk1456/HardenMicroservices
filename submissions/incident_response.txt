1. Incident commander name:
Ben

2. Executive Summary of what happened:
Three pods were created that ran 3 different crypto miners: cryptotonight, minergate, and moneropool.

3. Summary of what was affected: 
The CPU utilization spiked precipitously.

4. Summmry of the impact:
The three pods utilized resources that could have been used by legitimate pods. Moreover, those three pods ran software that wasn't vetted and could introduce further vulnerabilities into the system.

5. Summary of the remediation steps:
The three pods were evicted.

6. Summary of lessons learned:
Alerts should be created that trigger when CPU resources rise precipitously. The controllers that admit containers should be configured to block unknown images from creating containers.
